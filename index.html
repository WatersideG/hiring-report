Signal 4: Intangibles & Trajectory (0-25 points)

M2: Content & Channel Strategy (0-20 points)

M3: Data & Analytics Fluency (0-20 points)

Python for marketing automation and analysis, SQL for data extraction, data visualization (Looker, Tableau, GA4 dashboards), A/B testing methodology, attribution modeling, conversion funnel analysis.

M4: Experience & Execution (0-20 points)

Duration and quality of marketing-specific roles. Campaign management, marketing automation workflow design, paid media budget management, lead generation execution, cross-functional marketing operations.

M5: Intangibles & Growth (0-15 points)

Google Ads/HubSpot/Meta certifications, marketing community engagement, portfolio of campaigns or content, career trajectory toward marketing technology, adaptability and learning velocity.
